TICKET CATEGORY: SHIPPING & DELIVERY INFORMATION

1. Shipping Methods & Speed:
- Standard Shipping: Cost is $4.99, or free for orders over $50. Delivery takes 3 to 5 business days within the continental United States.
- Express Shipping: Cost is $14.99. Delivery takes 1 to 2 business days. Orders must be placed before 2:00 PM EST for same-day dispatch.
- International Shipping: Available to over 50 countries. Cost ranges from $20.00 to $45.00 depending on location. Delivery takes 7 to 14 business days. Custom duties, taxes, and import fees are the responsibility of the recipient.

2. Tracking Orders:
- Once an order is dispatched, a tracking number is automatically emailed to the customer.
- Tracking numbers may take up to 24 hours to become active on the carrier's (UPS, FedEx, USPS) website.
- If a package is marked as "Delivered" but the customer hasn't received it, we advise checking with neighbors, scanning the property, or waiting 24 hours, as carriers sometimes mark items delivered prematurely. If it is still missing, notify support within 5 days.

3. Restructured or Delayed Deliveries:
- Weather, peak holiday seasons, or carrier bottlenecks can cause shipments to be delayed. Standard procedures require waiting 3 additional business days beyond the estimated delivery date before filing a missing package investigation.
