TICKET CATEGORY: REFUNDS & RETURNS POLICY

1. Overview:
Customers can return any product within 30 days of receipt for a full refund or exchange, provided the item is in its original packaging, unused, and with all tags intact.

2. Refund Eligibility:
- Standard Items: Eligible for full refund within the 30-day window.
- Clearance/Sale Items: All sales are final. No refunds or exchanges are permitted unless the product is defective upon receipt.
- Digital Products: Once downloaded or accessed, digital products or software licenses are non-refundable.

3. Process & Timelines:
- Return Shipping: Free return shipping labels are provided for items returned due to company error (e.g., incorrect item shipped, defective item). For general returns, the customer covers the return shipping cost ($5.99 deducted from the refund).
- Processing Time: Once the returned item is received and inspected at our warehouse, refunds are processed within 5 to 7 business days.
- Credit Posting: The refund will be credited back to the customer's original payment method. Depending on the financial institution, it may take an additional 2-5 business days to post to the account.

4. Cancelled Orders:
Orders can be cancelled free of charge if they have not yet entered the shipping phase (usually within 1 hour of placing the order). If the order has already shipped, the customer must initiate a standard return.
